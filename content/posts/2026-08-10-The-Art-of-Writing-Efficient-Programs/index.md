Here are my personal notes from **Fedor G. Pikus’ *The Art of Writing Efficient Programs***.

This book explores the principles and techniques behind writing high-performance software, with a strong focus on modern CPUs, memory architecture, concurrency, C++ optimization, compiler behavior, and performance-oriented software design.

<i>I am still reading the book, so these notes are currently incomplete and are a work in progress. I will continue updating this page as I read through the remaining chapters and learn more from the book.</i>

You can download the (incomplete) notes in PDF format [here](./TheArtOfWritingEfficientProgramsNotes.pdf).

**Book:** *The Art of Writing Efficient Programs*
**Author:** Fedor G. Pikus
**Publisher:** Packt Publishing
**Reference:** [O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-art-of/9781800208117/)

The notes are intended as a concise personal reference for understanding and applying performance optimization techniques in C++ and systems programming.
